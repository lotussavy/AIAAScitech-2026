Bundled hourly summaries, spatial CSVs, and historical cost outputs were supplied with the author's project. Their provenance and known gaps are documented in [docs/DATA.md](docs/DATA.md). Consult the original [NYC TLC data source](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page) for source terms and attribution. No separate third-party dataset license was supplied with these CSVs; this file does not invent one.

The paper PDF is not bundled. Citation metadata and a description of the methods are included for research attribution. Retain data provenance and the known-discrepancy notes when sharing derived results.
